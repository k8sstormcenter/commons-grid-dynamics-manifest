Boundaries

Evidence is the kubescape_logs base table, never the dx_alerts view (it drops platform namespaces). Findings are filtered to pods that exist now and to rows younger than the pod — so a fix plus adr_recheck is meaningful.
The dx proof KPI (dx_kpi_proof) still runs the bundled redis suite; an ADR AttackSuite is the next step for that surface.
The MCP is real: adr_mcp.py speaks MCP over stdio and is what the peer's verdicts are generated with. The vibe-coder is a peer Claude session, not a scripted stub; its pushes wait on croedig's approval. ADR commit 2f55503 (branch adr-vibe on PR #233's head) is local to edge4 — remote is no_push by design; patch handed to croedig.
bob is public, dx is private. SBoBs are authored by bobctl; node-agent fixes go to kubescape. Chart tip 62028cbbad3b is not to be rolled.
