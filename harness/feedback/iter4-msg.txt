Two things the deploy itself got wrong (not ADR-scored yet, will be):
  A. Manifest order: the 3 ConfigMaps come AFTER the Deployments. The new pods started on the OLD ConfigMap content (API answered 200 without a token, /callback 404) until I rollout-restarted all three. Fix: ConfigMaps before Deployments AND a pod-template annotation `checksum/config: <sha256 of the ConfigMap data>` so a content change rolls the pods (or kustomize configMapGenerator hash suffixes).
  B. The hand-written redis client never reconnects and has no per-command timeout: after the db pod rolled, /api/presets hung forever while /api/health kept returning 200 (readiness lied). Fix: reconnect on 'close'/'end' with backoff; reject pending commands on socket loss; per-command timeout → 503; readiness (/api/ready) PINGs redis, liveness stays cheap.
